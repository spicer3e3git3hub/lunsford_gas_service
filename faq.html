Frequently asked questions

The questions we get asked most.

I smell gas near my fireplace. What should I do?

If the smell is strong, leave the house right away, do not flip any light switches or use anything that could spark, and call 911 or your gas utility's emergency line from outside. Once you are safe, call us. Do not wait on a web form for anything that smells like an active leak.

What areas do you serve?

Homeowners throughout Davidson County and the surrounding counties in middle Tennessee. If you are unsure about your address, call us before submitting a request.

Are your products made in the USA?

Yes. We sell and install only American-made products.

What is the difference between vented and vent free gas logs?

Vented logs are made to look like a real wood fire and need an open chimney flue. Vent free logs run with the damper closed and are made to heat the room. We can help you decide which fits your fireplace.

Do gas logs need regular maintenance?

Yes. Having them inspected once a year, ideally before the weather turns cold, helps catch problems early.

When do you build fire pits?

In the warmer months, we provide custom gas fire pits and fire pit service.

What outdoor kitchen work do you do?

We install outdoor kitchen equipment from more than 10 manufacturers.
