About Lunsford Gas Service

Lunsford Gas Service is a family-owned small business led by Mark Lunsford, specializing in gas log installation and service. With more than 30 years of experience, Mark and his hands-on team provide dependable, straightforward service for homeowners throughout Davidson County and the surrounding counties in middle Tennessee. We sell and install only American-made products. In the warmer months, we also provide custom gas fire pits and fire pit service, along with installation of outdoor kitchen equipment from more than 10 manufacturers.
